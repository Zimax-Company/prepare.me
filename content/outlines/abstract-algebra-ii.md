ABSTRACT ALGEBRA II
Level 1: Advanced Group Theory
Objective: Study deeper structural properties of groups and their actions on mathematical objects.
Module	Topics
1.1	Review of group structures
1.2	Advanced subgroup theory
1.3	Group actions
1.4	Orbits and stabilisers
1.5	Orbit-stabiliser theorem
1.6	Conjugation actions
1.7	Class equation
1.8	Centre of a group
1.9	Centralisers and normalisers
1.10	Sylow theorems
1.11	Applications of Sylow theorems
1.12	Classification of finite groups (introduction)
1.13	Simple groups
1.14	Solvable groups
1.15	Group extensions (introduction)

Expected competencies:
- Analyse groups through their actions.
- Apply orbit-stabiliser relationships.
- Understand conjugacy and class equations.
- Apply Sylow theorems to classify finite groups.
- Understand simple and solvable groups.
Assessment: Advanced group classification and theorem-based proofs.

Level 2: Advanced Group Structures
Objective: Explore constructions and relationships between more complex groups.
Module	Topics
2.1	Direct products of groups
2.2	Semidirect products
2.3	Group presentations
2.4	Generators and relations
2.5	Free groups
2.6	Permutation representations
2.7	Automorphism groups
2.8	Inner automorphisms
2.9	Group extensions
2.10	Classification of finitely generated Abelian groups
2.11	Fundamental theorem of finite Abelian groups
2.12	Applications to symmetry and abstract structures

Expected competencies:
- Construct complex groups from simpler groups.
- Understand groups defined by generators and relations.
- Analyse automorphism groups.
- Classify finite Abelian groups.
- Understand group representations.
Assessment: Group construction, classification and structural proofs.

Expected competencies:
- Identify and construct ideals.
- Distinguish maximal and prime ideals.
- Construct quotient rings.
- Apply the Chinese remainder theorem.
- Understand ring homomorphisms and their kernels.
Assessment: Ideal construction, quotient ring problems and theorem proofs.

- Apply irreducibility criteria.
- Understand polynomial factorisation over different fields.

- Understand free and cyclic modules.
- Apply structure theorems.
Assessment: Module construction and classification exercises.

Level 7: Galois Theory
Objective: Understand the relationship between polynomial equations, field extensions and groups of symmetries.

This is arguably one of the most important and intellectually rewarding areas of classical abstract algebra.
Module	Topics
7.1	Introduction to Galois theory
7.2	Automorphisms of field extensions
7.3	Galois groups
7.4	Fixed fields
7.5	Galois extensions
7.6	Fundamental theorem of Galois theory
7.7	Galois correspondence
7.8	Galois groups of polynomials
7.9	Solvability by radicals
7.10	Solvable groups and polynomial equations
7.11	Classical theory of polynomial equations
7.12	Abel-Ruffini theorem
7.13	Applications of Galois theory

Expected competencies:
- Determine automorphisms of field extensions.
- Construct and analyse Galois groups.
- Understand the correspondence between subgroups and intermediate fields.
- Determine whether certain polynomials are solvable by radicals.
- Understand the mathematical significance of the Abel-Ruffini theorem.
Assessment: Galois group calculations, field extension problems and theorem proofs.
